*Version 3, research date: 8 October 2026*

- Contact details are included only where a search result actually showed them. 'Not found' means exactly that. I have not guessed or filled in numbers. Several numbers come from older admission notices or third-party directories and are marked; confirm them before use.

These are institutionally more credible, but you must still ask each for its programme approval letter. Private caregiver academies whose accreditation I could not verify are moved to the 'excluded' table in section 11.
- You asked for accredited ONLINE schools in Ghana and Benin Republic. I found none that I can verify. Details in sections 5 and 6. I will not present unverified online providers as accredited.
- Correction to the earlier version: the college at oscohtechilese.edu.ng is most likely the Ogun State college at Ilese-Ijebu (an Ogun State Polytechnic of Health and Allied Sciences page describes the Ilese-Ijebu institution as NBTE-regulated), not an Osun college as I labelled it. This is an inference from the domain and search results, not confirmed.

## 2. Executive conclusion

- No school in any of the four countries can be called 'WES-approved'. WES evaluates an individual's documents after the institution sends them directly.
- For PSW-type work in Ontario the more decisive benchmark is a programme of at least 600 hours (class plus practical) under O. Reg. 217/24, with employers judging equivalency themselves under O. Reg. 471/24. Other provinces differ.
- Nigeria: the best-supported options are public health-technology college Health Assistant programmes (about 2 years where stated) and teaching-hospital caregiver certificates (RSUTH 3 months, LASUTH). Check the programme approval letter.
- Ghana: the Nurse Assistant (Clinical) and (Preventive) programmes were ordered suspended from 2019/2020 by the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana (2018 report). Later pages still list them, so the current status is unclear. No verified accredited online caregiver school found.
- Benin Republic: no verified accredited online school found. Benin has tightened recognition: foreign private health schools' diplomas are only eligible for equivalence if the school is on a 2025 decree list. Public institutions such as INMeS (Cotonou) are the credible leads, and aide-soignant track availability is unconfirmed.
- UK: there are many Ofqual-regulated online Level 2 and Level 3 care diplomas, but they require a work placement in a UK care setting, which is hard to arrange from abroad. Since 22 July 2025 the UK has closed overseas recruitment for care worker (SOC 6145) visas, so a UK care qualification is not a route to UK care-worker employment from Nigeria, and a UK Level 3 is not automatically a PSW equivalent in Canada.

## 4. Nigeria: detail and justification

Programmes: see the earlier version for school-by-school curricula. Updated points from this round:

- Osun State College of Health Technology, Ilesa: Health Assistant listed as 2 years / four semesters; 5 credits (English, Maths, Biology or Health Science, Chemistry, a pass in Physics) in at most 2 sittings; entrance exam and interview; UTME reportedly compulsory in 2026/27. Application portal at osunhealthtechilesa.edu.ng. No phone or email found; check the site's contact page.
- Lead City University Hospital & College of Health Technology: two-semester Certified Healthcare Assistant; 5 O-level credits including English; form ₦10,000 and ₦75,000 per semester on the page seen. The university page lists LCU Hospital 0815 331 8767 and Registrar 0815 331 8702. No dedicated email found.
- LASUTH Caregiver Academy: screening is a computer-based test plus oral interview; the academy graduated its fourth cohort on 21 May 2026, so it is active. Numbers are from a 2024 notice.
- RSUTH CENHACT: 3-month, 16-module programme, ages 20-55, online application via rsuth.ng. Fees not found.
- LASCOHET has warned the public about admission racketeers. Apply only through its official portal.
- Accreditation: the NMCN lists approved nursing schools but I found no NMCN pathway for caregiver or assistant courses. Ask each institution for the NBTE or other board approval letter for the exact programme and verify it with the board.

Why these and not the private academies: public and teaching-hospital bodies are answerable to government regulators, issue records you can ask the registrar to send to WES, and are less likely to disappear. This is a probability judgement, not proof of programme accreditation.

## 5. Ghana: detail and justification

- Regulator: Nursing and Midwifery Council of Ghana (NMC), phone +233 302 522 909 / 10, info@nmc.gov.gh, No. 30 Adamafio Crescent, Okponglo, East Legon, Accra. Ask it in writing which schools are currently approved for NAC/NAP and for any online provider.
- Status of the cadre: a 2018 news report says NMC suspended Nurse Assistant (Clinical) and (Preventive) training from the 2019/2020 year. A 2026/27 admissions guide still lists NAC/NAP among auxiliary programmes, and Christian Service University is running a bridging course for existing certificate holders. Treat NAC/NAP as uncertain until the NMC confirms.
- Online options found: CES nursing assistants CPD (no accreditor named); Ransford Global 12-week online certificate in healthcare assistance (no accreditation stated); Succesfield College 'advanced diploma' (claims approval and accreditation by two Zambian colleges; not credible, excluded). None qualifies as 'accredited online school' on the evidence.
- In-person caregiver option: Comfort Hands certified caregiver training in Accra, 4 days, GHS 1,000, certificate of completion (listing only; contact not found).
- Warning: the NMC revoked accreditation of Ghana Christian University College for all professional nursing and midwifery programmes. Check status before enrolling anywhere.

## 6. Benin Republic: detail and justification

- I found no verified accredited online aide-soignant or caregiver school. Do not enrol in an online Beninese provider without a ministry approval number.
- Public leads: INMeS Cotonou (inmes.uac@uac.bj), attached to the Université d'Abomey-Calavi, mainly trains state nurses and midwives; ask whether an aide-soignant track exists. EFMS Parakou is referenced for aides-soignants. Entry for aide-soignant training in West Africa is typically BEPC level at a health school recognised by the Ministry of Health, per a regional guide (not a Beninese rule).
- Private schools named in job profiles (Morning Academy, La Pyramide Innovante) have no accreditation evidence and are excluded.
- Rule to know: Benin's 2023 decree limits equivalence for private foreign health diplomas to schools on a decree list (adopted 2025; sources cite decree 2025-080 and 2025-090). Students at schools not on the list face automatic refusal on return. Check the decree text on sgg.gouv.bj or ask the Dges.
- For French-language documents, expect WES to need certified translations; confirm WES's rules for the country before paying.

## 7. United Kingdom: detail and justification

- What exists: Ofqual-regulated Level 2 and Level 3 Diplomas in Care / Adult Care delivered online by many providers, awarded by bodies such as NCFE CACHE, TQUK, Focus Awards and QUALIFI. The Level 3 NCFE CACHE Diploma in Adult Care has 580 total qualification time hours and 58 credits (603/2618/9). Another provider lists the same title as 610/7192/4 with 50 hours of placement. Qualification numbers and credits differ between providers, so compare the exact reference on the Ofqual Register.
- Placement is the catch: almost all require paid or voluntary work in a care setting to complete assessment (for example 7-9 hours per week at one provider). Learners outside the UK rarely can meet this, so a purely online completion from Nigeria is doubtful. Ask each provider whether overseas learners can use a placement in another country.
- Free UK distance courses at colleges such as Telford and Wiltshire are for England residents (Telford: aged 19+), so they are not available to you from abroad.
- Immigration: the Skilled Worker (Health and Care) route was closed to new overseas care worker (SOC 6145) recruitment on 22 July 2025; people already in the UK can extend or switch until 2028. A UK care diploma therefore will not by itself lead to a UK care-worker job from Nigeria.
- Canada: a UK Level 3 may be assessed as a credential but is not automatically a PSW equivalent. The 600-hour Ontario benchmark counts class plus practical time, and 'total qualification time' includes self-study, so providers' hour figures may not map directly. Ask the employer or regulator.
- Accreditation check: confirm the qualification number on the Ofqual Register of Regulated Qualifications and ask the provider which awarding body it is an approved centre for. I could not verify any provider's centre status. Contacts for most online providers were not found in search; use their websites' contact pages.

## 8. Canada / WES requirements recap

- WES cannot request documents for you; the institution must send them. Applicant-sent copies are generally not accepted unless WES allows it for the country.
- Mail documents in a sealed, stamped or signed envelope with your WES reference number; some institutions send electronically.
- WES generally evaluates postsecondary credentials. I could not confirm that short caregiver certificates are assessable. Ask WES support quoting the exact award name.
- Ontario PSW: 600-hour programme benchmark (O. Reg. 217/24); long-term-care employers decide equivalency (O. Reg. 471/24). Other provinces have their own HCA/PSW standards.

## 9. Due-diligence checklist

- Exact award name and level.
- Awarding body, approving regulator and written approval or accreditation letter with a reference number; verify it with the regulator.
- Total hours, split into classroom, lab, and supervised practical hours.
- Transcript with course titles, hours and grades.
- Will the school send records directly to WES, how, at what fee, and how fast?
- For online providers: where is the supervised placement done, who supervises, and who signs it off?
- Current intake, fees and dates for 2026/27 in writing.
- Employer or provincial regulator opinion on whether the credential is acceptable.
