## 2. Executive conclusion

- No Nigerian caregiver or nursing-assistant programme is 'WES-approved' in advance. WES assesses an individual's credential after documents are received. The institution must send the documents itself; WES cannot request them for you and an applicant-sent copy is generally not accepted unless WES allows it for the country.
- For Ontario PSW-type work, the benchmark that matters most is not WES but Ontario's PSW registration standard: a programme of at least 600 hours including class and practical time (O. Reg. 217/24). Long-term-care employers must hire people who are registered or who meet that standard, and they make the equivalency judgement themselves. A short 1-12 week Nigerian caregiver course is very unlikely to meet 600 hours.
- The most defensible Nigerian options found are therefore the longer, institution-backed ones: Health Assistant programmes at state health-technology colleges (about 2 years / 4 semesters where stated) and the Lead City University Hospital Certified Healthcare Assistant (two semesters). Teaching-hospital caregiver certificates (RSUTH 3 months, LASUTH) are credible for skills but short.
- Private 'international diploma' caregiver courses (days to a few months) are skills training. Do not pay for them expecting a WES or Ontario outcome.
- Nigeria has no unified national caregiver certification yet. The Federal Government announced in May 2026 plans to scale up caregiver training and certification, the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC) is still developing a National Care Agenda for 2027, and the National Senior Citizens Centre has been developing an elderly-caregiver curriculum with NBTE. Standards may change soon.

## 4. Detailed information and justification

### 4.1 Tier 1: longer, institution-backed routes (best to investigate)

Lead City University Hospital & College of Health Technology, Ibadan. The college describes a Certified Healthcare Assistant programme over two semesters covering medical terminology, anatomy and physiology, pharmacology, patient care, and medical coding and billing. Entry is five O-level credits including English. The page showed an application form at ₦10,000 and tuition of ₦75,000 per semester. Justification: it is hospital-linked and curriculum-based, which makes it more credible than a stand-alone training centre. Limits: two semesters is well short of 600 hours of PSW-style training unless the practical hours are large, the page I saw is dated to a January 2024 start, and 'medical coding and billing' is not PSW content. Ask for total contact and clinical hours.

Osun State College of Health Technology, Ilesa. The 2023/24 listing gives Health Assistant as a two-year (four-semester) course, with five credits (English, Maths, Biology or Health Science, Chemistry, a pass in Physics) in at most two sittings, an entrance exam and an interview. A 2026/27 summary says UTME/JAMB is now compulsory. Justification: a public health-technology college and the longest duration verified for a health-assistant route. Limits: I found no official 2026/27 prospectus, no fee table, and no description of clinical hours. Confirm the exact award and whether it is recognised by the relevant board before enrolling.

College at oscohtechilese.edu.ng. A programme page gives Health Assistant as 2 years with three credits (Biology, Chemistry, Maths) plus a pass in English, and says Health Assistant graduates enter year 2 of the 3-year Health Technician programme. Justification: the progression to a longer technician award is useful if you want a more substantial credential later. Limit: the exact college name could not be confirmed from the excerpt.

### 4.2 Tier 2: hospital-run caregiver certificates (credible skills training, short)

RSUTH CENHACT (Port Harcourt). A 3-month programme with sixteen core modules and hands-on sessions, focused on elderly and dependent persons. Eligibility is age 20-55 with online application. Contact details published by the centre are cenhact@rsuth.ng, phones 07068300655 and 08066992304. Justification: a teaching-hospital setting and elderly-care focus map closely to PSW tasks. Limits: about 3 months is far below 600 hours; the 'recognised for global opportunities' wording is marketing; fees and current intake were not found.

LASUTH Caregiver Academy (Lagos). Applicants submit credentials, a CV, passport photos and a WAEC/SSCE/NECO result, then sit a computer-based test and an oral interview. The registration deadline found was 31 October 2025, so check for a 2026 intake. Justification: a government teaching-hospital brand. Limits: duration, fees and curriculum were not found.

### 4.3 Tier 3: private skills courses (low WES/Ontario value)

Clarion College, CIEL, SkillBoost, Deslog, Utopian Healthcare, GoCare and similar providers publish prices and short durations (5 days to about 6 months). Clarion lists ₦70,000 (hybrid, about one week), ₦150,000 (practical-only, about four weeks), ₦230,000 (with a 1-3 month internship) and a ₦350,000 diploma bundle, plus a clinical reference letter from a partner hospital. These can help with employment preparation and a reference, but the credentials are issued by private providers and none cited a Nigerian regulator. A course called a 'diploma' is not thereby a postsecondary diploma.

### 4.4 Regulators and standards: what exists

- The Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) accredits nursing and midwifery schools. I found no NMCN accreditation pathway for caregiver or nursing-assistant courses, only a public disclaimer about one unaccredited nursing college. Do not accept 'NMCN approved' for a caregiver course without seeing the approval letter.
- NBTE oversees technical institutions. Health-technology colleges typically run under NBTE and professional boards, which is why those colleges are stronger than private academies. Confirm which body approves each specific programme.
- In 2018 reporting, Oyo state objected to private-facility auxiliary-nurse training and Ogun state reportedly banned auxiliary nurses. This is old and may have changed, but it shows that local rules vary by state.
- In May 2026 the Federal Government (Ministry of Women Affairs) announced plans to scale up caregiver training and certification. The NIC calls 2026 a preparatory year and is drafting a National Care Agenda for 2027. The National Senior Citizens Centre has been developing a national elderly-caregiver curriculum with NBTE. No unified national framework existed as of the sources found.

## 5. Canada and WES: what actually decides the outcome

### 5.1 WES

- WES cannot request documents from your institution; the institution must send them. Documents sent by the applicant are generally not accepted unless WES allows it for your country.
- Mailed documents must be in a sealed envelope signed or stamped across the flap, with your WES reference number. Nigerian universities typically courier these (DHL/FedEx). One guide estimates 2-5 months for major federal institutions; start early.
- Some institutions send electronically. Ask whether your school already works with WES.
- Ask the school for a WES Academic Records Request Form workflow and fee in advance.
- I could not confirm from WES's own pages whether a short caregiver certificate is eligible for a Canadian ECA. WES generally evaluates postsecondary credentials. Check the Required Documents page for Nigeria and, if in doubt, ask WES support before paying a school.

### 5.2 Ontario PSW (the more important test)

- Under O. Reg. 217/24, a registrable PSW programme is at least 600 hours including class and practical time. Registration is with the Health and Supportive Care Providers Oversight Authority.
- Under O. Reg. 471/24, long-term-care licensees must hire people who are registered or whom the licensee determines meet the registration requirements. Employers therefore judge equivalency, typically from hours and content, not from a WES label.
- Transitional dates in the sources (1 January 2026 and 1 February 2026) have passed. Check current e-Laws text.
- Implication: choose a programme with documented total hours of at least 600 (class plus supervised practical) and a transcript showing courses and hours. A WES report may help, but employers and the Oversight Authority decide.

Caveat: these sources describe Ontario. Other provinces (for example BC Health Care Assistant, Alberta HCA) have their own rules. Check the target province before choosing.

## 7. Due-diligence checklist (ask in writing before paying)

- Exact award name and level (certificate, diploma, ND, attendance certificate).
- Awarding body and approving regulator, with the approval letter or accreditation number.
- Total hours: classroom, laboratory, and supervised clinical placement, separately.
- Is a transcript with course titles, hours and grades issued?
- Will the school send documents directly to WES (courier or electronic), at what fee and lead time?
- Has the school sent records to WES or Canadian colleges before?
- Is the programme currently running, and what are the 2026/27 fees and dates?
- Does the curriculum cover personal care, mobility and transfers, infection control, nutrition, dementia and palliative care, documentation and communication?
- Can you speak to a past graduate who works abroad?
