CONSTRAINTS (STEP TWO)
Quality: status meanings not yet agreed; some needs in free text; floor plans may be out of date
Frequency: human-generated and intermittent, one request at a time; updates at each decision
Format: form fields, attachments, email, Teams, phone
Ownership: Keep and Shape owner and data owner Open
Access: to confirm; Proposed: a CMAC sees only their own requests
Retention: to confirm with client
TRUSTED MEANS
one Request ID; agreed status, one meaning; current responsible role or group; information needed for the next step; dates in true order; duplicates checked on an agreed key. A check confirms presence, not accuracy.
RECORD HOLDS
Request ID; requester, CMAC, ministry; need, quantity, location; attachments; status and responsible role; decisions, ownership changes, dated updates with author; proposal, approval, delivery, closure kept as separate records.

CONTROLS ON EACH HAND-OFF (SESSION FOUR): authenticate; validate; monitor; mitigate; alarm
Generate to Collect: authorized requester only; required fields and documents by request type; needs left in email or Teams stay invisible; drafts saved; submission confirmed
Collect to Keep and Shape: reviewing roles only (to confirm); completeness checked, incomplete requests return to the CMAC; time to first assignment; original kept; CMAC told when action is required
Keep and Shape to Store: only coordinators change status or merge duplicates (Proposed); standard status list and duplicate check (key to define); every stage change time-stamped and attributed; a merged duplicate keeps both histories; coordinator alerted to a possible duplicate (Proposed)
Store to Use: role-based access (to confirm); views read the trusted record only; a request with no stage change is flagged (threshold to define); a failed update is retried and the user told (Proposed); stalled request visible to the coordinator (Proposed)
Use to the sinks: reports aggregated and role-restricted; figures traceable to the record; report freshness on a set period (to confirm); retention per the records schedule (to confirm); an unanswered Action Required item emails the requester
