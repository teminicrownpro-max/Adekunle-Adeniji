TRUSTED MEANS (KEEP AND SHAPE)
One Request ID; an agreed status with one meaning; the current responsible role or group; the information needed for the next step; dates in true order; duplicates checked on an agreed key (Open). The original stays as submitted. A check proves presence, not accuracy.
THE SHARED RECORD HOLDS (STORE)
Request ID; requester, CMAC and ministry; item, quantity, reason, location; attachments; status and responsible role; decisions, ownership changes and dated updates with author; proposal, approval, delivery and closure kept as separate records. Added to, never overwritten.

Dimensions
Headwind and tailwind
Implication
G1 Gap
Generate to Collect
Input reliability: required plans, dimensions and supporting documents may be missing or unreliable. Includes incomplete request information, floor-plan currentness and unclear contact purposes.
Data, process, governance
Headwind: found only after submission; plan upkeep sits beyond the form. Tailwind: review practice shows what is needed.
Change the workflow: confirm requirements by request type and check completeness earlier. Floor-plan upkeep: phase it or scope it out.
L1 Leak
Collect to Store
Connected request history: updates and decisions are spread across email, Teams and phone and do not all reach the request.
Process, organizational readiness
Headwind: updates span people and channels. Tailwind: some records are already kept.
Change the workflow: agree which material updates belong in the history and who records them.
L2 Potential leak
Store to Use
Information hand-offs: supplied information may not reach the next responsible person, for example delivery contacts asked for again; a failed notification is not visible to the sender.
Process, organizational readiness, data
Headwind: onward transfer is inconsistent; the sender cannot see a failed hand-off. Tailwind: contacts are already supplied.
Change the workflow: assign who transfers delivery details and checks receipt. Phase it: failure feedback and recovery.
V1 To validate
Keep and Shape to Store to Use
Measurement readiness: event coverage, meanings and access are not verified. Includes stage-duration measurement and reusable requirement structure.
Data, process
Headwind: event meanings unverified; free text needs interpretation. Tailwind: existing dates are a starting point.
Phase it: check existing history first; define prospective capture for missing events.
