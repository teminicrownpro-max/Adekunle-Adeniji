Next steps for the PRD

  1. Confirm business rules and authoritative sources with the GoA owners.
  2. Trace representative records through the pipeline, including hand-offs outside the product.
  3. Confirm ownership and access for record quality, updates and sharing.
  4. Validate measurement feasibility: event coverage and collection methods.
  5. Carry confirmed requirements into the PRD; keep unresolved dependencies as explicit conditions.
