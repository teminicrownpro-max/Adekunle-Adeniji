Data sources and where they are born

3 / 12

We separate where information is first created from where it is later communicated or stored. The original requester creates the furniture need; the CMAC prepares and submits the formal request.

SourceData elements it providesWhere it is bornHow it enters the pipeline
Original requester
person		Furniture need, quantity, location, intended user, special requirements, budget and contact informationIn the requester's own work, when a furniture need is identifiedTold to the CMAC by email, Teams or phone; enters the service only when the CMAC submits
CMAC
person		Submission details, supporting documents, requester and site-contact information, clarification responses, proposal feedbackThe submission workflow, acting for the original requesterThe guided request form and replies in the service
Furniture Request Coordinator
Infrastructure staff		Intake decisions, assignments, clarification requests, final review and closure decisionsThe intake, assignment and final-review workflowRecorded as case activity on the request
Furniture Coordinator
Infrastructure staff		Fulfilment updates, proposed furniture options, coordination decisions, approval status, vendor instructions, next actionsThe fulfilment and coordination workflowRecorded as case activity on the request
Vendor or supplier
third party		Quotations, furniture availability, delivery schedules, delivery confirmations, installation updates, outstanding issuesSupply, delivery and installation work outside governmentThrough correspondence; enters only when staff capture it in the service
PRISM Requests
system, proposed		Request ID, timestamps, assignments, status history, case activity, closure timestampCreated by the service as people use itRecorded automatically on the request
