Measurement and business sinks, and the PRD

M1 Product outcome measurement

Type
Measurement sink, tied to the DPO
Consumer
Product or service owner and authorized evaluators
Measures
DVO 1 first-pass submission completeness. DVO 2 status comprehension, information retrieval and follow-up effort. DVO 6 manual reporting effort.
Value
Evidence of whether the product improves its intended outcomes; targets not yet set
Storage and access
Fresh
Per evaluation period
Complete
Consistent definitions, reliable dates, comparable periods; a follow-up baseline including communication outside the system
Form
One measure per DVO, with recorded evaluation results
Who sees
Authorized evaluators

B1 Operational reporting

Type
Business-value sink, beyond the product
Consumer
Authorized Infrastructure management
Data used
Request volumes, stage, age, completion trends and recorded hand-offs
Value
Identify bottlenecks, understand workload and support operational improvements
Storage and access
Fresh
Current enough for decisions (period to confirm)
Complete
Reliable counts, consistent stage definitions, stage-change timestamps, completion dates and history
Form
Aggregated reports
Who sees
Authorized Infrastructure management

What goes into the PRD and Milestone 3

  • P1 to P3: define what CMACs and coordinators see, and how updates, missing information and changes of responsibility are shown.
  • M1: define how DVO 1, 2 and 6 are measured, what evidence is needed and what counts as success.
  • B1: define the operational questions management needs answered and what the reports must provide.
  • Milestone 3: decide how current and historical data is stored, maintained, kept accurate and made available to the right users.

Still to confirm: coordinator hand-offs, mandatory fields, status definitions, user permissions, measurement baselines and management reporting needs.
